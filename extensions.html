What the v1.0 surface could not answer

v1.0 declared outcome S1 from a 75-point (width, depth, steps) surface capped at T = 16. The v1.1 extensions push past every boundary that cap imposed: steps to T = 64, allocation across codebook levels, a fourth parameter budget, a training-compute control, and a robustness panel over the metric stack. Six hypotheses were pre-registered before any extension datum existed.

Where it stands Five of nine jobs are complete. H-E3 is supported — reallocating a fixed inference budget to the coarsest codebook level cuts WER 5.2×. H-E1 is half-supported and delivered the project's strongest single result: identity saturates at T*=8 while intelligibility keeps improving to T*=32, making the headline claim fit-free. H-E2 is refuted, and a re-run on the extended grid overturned my own first interpretation of why. v1.0 remains frozen and untouched throughout.

§2E1 — test-time scaling past the v1.0 cap

21 runs (A3, B3, C1–C5 × 3 seeds) × T ∈ {1,2,4,8,16,24,32,64} on 200 eval items. Every T is computed over the same 200 items: the T ≤ 16 points are re-aggregated read-only from v1.0's per-item rows rather than reused at 400 items, so the curve measures the T range and not a change of subset.

The result that matters T*SIM = 8 while T*WER = 32 — a 4× gap in saturation step, on the same runs and the same items. From T=16 to T=64, WER improves 15% relative while SIM-o improves 0.5%. The project's S1 claim — refinement steps buy intelligibility, not identity — no longer rests on the fitted exchange rate Δτ; it is now two directly measured saturation points. This is the strongest single piece of evidence in the project.
Pre-registered half that failed τWER refitted on the extended range is 0.9527, CI [0.9371, 0.9681] — disjoint from the v1.0 CI [0.8248, 0.8513], so H-E1 as a conjunction is not supported. A pre-planned control isolates why: refitting on the same 7 configs and same 200 items but only T ≤ 16 gives τ = 0.8773 CI [0.8632, 0.8933]. The shift decomposes into a subset effect of ≈ +0.03 and a dominant range effect of ≈ +0.075. v1.0's τ was mildly under-estimated by truncation at T=16 — not biased by the subset.

§3E3 — where you spend the steps matters as much as how many

15 C-budget runs × 3 frozen per-level schedules at matched total NFE = 32, full 400 items, 18 000 item-rows. Paired item-level bootstrap (2 000 replicates, RNG 7331); pairing is on the eval item, so item difficulty cancels.

Primary: WER(coarse) − WER(fine) = −0.9289, CI [−0.9595, −0.8999]. Secondary: WER(coarse) − WER(uniform) = −0.0953, CI [−0.1044, −0.0870]. Both supported. At identical inference cost, moving 32 forward passes from the finest codebook level to the coarsest changes WER by 5.2× and cuts the degenerate rate from 39.1% to 0.1%. Level 0 sets the phonetic content every later level conditions on; level 7 carries residual detail that no amount of iteration can use to repair a wrong coarse plan.

Calibration. Uniform at NFE=32 is T=4 per level, and E1 measures WER 0.321 at T=4 vs 0.191 at T=8. Coarse's 0.219 sits between them, so reallocating the same NFE to the coarse level buys roughly what 1.7× more uniformly-spent NFE would buy — for free.

Honest caveat — coarse is not a free win Against uniform, coarse wins WER by 0.095 but loses identity and perceptual quality, both with CIs excluding 0: SIM-o −0.0420 CI [−0.0461, −0.0378], UTMOS −0.3456 CI [−0.3589, −0.3311]. The same metric-selectivity the project reports for the amount of steps reappears in where they are spent. Labeled exploratory — the SIM/UTMOS contrasts were not pre-registered.

§4E2 — iso-latency allocation, and a corrected interpretation

Zero-GPU analysis over the frozen v1.0 surface with measured c_layer; serial latency L = 8·T·d·clayer, 40 log-spaced budgets, optimum found two independent ways (fitted Msep surface, and measured config×T means).

H-E2 is refuted on both grids (5.9% and 37%, against an 80% bar), so the pre-registered verdict is unchanged and now shown to be robust.

Correction — two errors of mine, both caught and fixed (1) The decision rule tested the wrong statistic. §9 asks whether the depth-first property holds under each method at ≥80% of budgets; the first implementation measured concordance between the methods, which is a fit-quality diagnostic, not the hypothesis. Under the correct rule H-E2 is refuted by a far wider margin (5.9%/8.8% vs an 80% bar) — the verdict never changed, the derivation did. (2) A published claim was false. I wrote that the measured optimum "never reaches the d* ridge"; it does — B5 (d=30) and C5 (d=36) at the two highest budgets. My table stopped short of them. (3) The replacement interpretation was itself a boundary artefact. On the capped grid steps maxed out at 726 ms while depth reached d* only at 2002 ms, which I described as "steps-first". On E1's extended grid the order reverses. The directional story does not survive and is not going in the paper. What survives: the optimum interleaves depth and steps rather than saturating depth first.

§5E5 — robustness of the fitted surface

The known v1.0 defect: M_full's width amplitude A hit its pre-registered upper bound (A = 10.000), leaving α — and therefore ρ = α/β and the H-D1 test — unidentified. The log-amplitude refit makes log A, log B, log C effectively unbounded and leaves exponent bounds exactly as pre-registered.

The pathology migrates rather than resolves Freeing the amplitude does move it off the bound (A = 3.4×105 for WER), but α then hits the exponent bound instead (α = 3.0000). For WER, α remains unidentified. For SIM it identifies cleanly (α = 1.566, no parameter at a bound). The diagnosis is the grid: width spans only 4.5× against depth's 9×, so A·w−α is a near-degenerate ridge — the product is determined, the factors are not. Widening the width axis, not re-parameterising, is the fix.

The headline survives the reparameterisation. Δτ = 0.1262 CI [0.1043, 0.1474] under log amplitudes, against v1.0's 0.1102 CI [0.0921, 0.1312] — same sign, overlapping CIs, both excluding 0. Δρ = −0.0516 CI [−0.4281, 0.3077] still contains 0, so H-D1 remains unsupported here too. Labeled exploratory-sensitivity: this cannot and does not rescue H-D1's pre-registered status.

§6Gates

E-AUDIT · pass 45/45 v1.0 checkpoints present and loadable at step 30 000. Smoke test: C3 seed 0 re-synthesised at T=16 → bit-identical tokens to the frozen v1.0 grids. Trap worth knowing: the first attempt reported 35.7% agreement because it synthesised a 5-item batch against v1.0's 50-item batches — the sampler's Gumbel draws are shaped [B, F, V], so batch composition is part of the RNG state. E1 therefore synthesises the first 200 items as v1.0's first four 50-item batches.
G1-D · fail → pre-registered remedy The 3 000-step D3 proxy at the μP-transferred base LR 0.004 reached val 5.6520 — below C3 (5.6893) but 0.0035 above B3 (5.6485), so the monotone-in-N check fails by a hair. §4-E4 pre-registers the remedy: one 5-point LR sweep at D3, adopt its argmin. The sweep gives a clean U-shape with a minimum at lr = 0.002 (val 5.5900) — below both B3 and C3, i.e. the gate passes at the adopted LR. μP transfer degraded slightly at 285M and the pre-registered sweep caught it.

§7Defects found and fixed during v1.1

An adversarial review of the extension code before any result was trusted found three real defects. All are recorded in full in LOG-v1.1.md; corrections are appended to RESULTS-FEED.md as new entries, never edits.

DefectImpactFix
P0-1 A leftover v1.0 scoring loop scored E1's 200-item dirs against the canonical 400-item list 200 phantom crashes per dir: WER 0.5634 recorded where the truth is 0.1268. 16 files. Compounded by the skip-if-exists check, which would have silently frozen the wrong file. Killed the loop; deleted all 16; score_dir now takes its denominator from the target dir's own synth.json and fails loud if absent. Behaviour-identical for v1.0 (all 225 dirs record 400). No v1.0 artifact was touched, and E1's own scores_ext200.json files were always correct.
P0-2 H-E2's decision rule implemented as the wrong statistic Reported 62% "agreement" as the test of a hypothesis about depth-first ordering. Decision statistic is now depth-first fraction under each method; concordance retained as a labeled secondary. Verdict unchanged.
P0-3 False claim in a published results entry ("never reaches the d* ridge") Contradicted by the analysis's own path CSV. Corrected as a new append-only entry; the artifact now records budgets_included and n_configs so scope limits travel with the number.
