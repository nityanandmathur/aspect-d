Denoising steps rent depth, not width

Per-metric (width, depth, steps) scaling laws for masked-diffusion TTS. Diffusion LMs have the one lever autoregressive models lack — serial compute purchasable at inference time. We test whether that lever moves every capability, or only the compositional ones.

TL;DR A masked-diffusion codec LM buys sequential computation two ways: depth d (paid in parameters, forever) and refinement steps T (paid per utterance, adjustable at serve time). We train one iso-parameter shape grid (20M/50M/125M × 5 aspect ratios, seeds ×2, +1 stretch), then sweep T ∈ {1…16} at evaluation only — one training grid yields a 75-point (w, d, T) response surface per metric for free. Pre-registered primary claims: H-D2 — steps improve intelligibility (WER) far more than speaker identity (SIM): test-time scaling is metric-selective; H-D3 — for WER, steps and depth are exchangeable at a measurable rate κ. Cost ≈240–420 GPU-h, capped 500, calendar-gated against the Aug 29 deadline. Every outcome (including the null) is a 4-page workshop paper. Outcomes (v1.0, measured): H-D2 supported in its declared coordinate — Δτ = +0.1102, CI [0.0921, 0.1312] — but Δτ is not coordinate-free: refitting the same measurements under log(1+err) or √err flips its sign, so v1.4 replaces it as the headline with a model-free, floor-referenced contrast (refinement closes 86.2% of the reachable intelligibility range against 46.4% of the reachable identity range, stable at 1.68–1.86× across those same maps). H-D1, H-D3 and H-D4 not supported; no κ is quotable. Full results in results.html, v1.1 extensions in extensions.html, the coordinate audit in coordinate-audit.html.

§1The question only a diffusion LM can ask

In an AR model, serial computation per emitted token is fixed at training time: it equals depth. Test-time compute can widen the search (sampling, reranking) but cannot add refinement passes over the sequence itself. A masked-diffusion LM decouples this: each unmasking iteration is one more full round of global, bidirectional conditioning — architecturally, one more "layer" of sequential composition, rented per utterance instead of bought in parameters.

TTS is the ideal probe because its quality axes plausibly live in different computational regimes. Intelligibility requires the compositional chain text → phonemes → durations → acoustics, and its characteristic failures (skips, repeats, garbled alignment) are failures of sequential planning that later refinement passes could correct. Speaker identity is representational — timbre lives in per-token channel capacity, and no amount of re-reading a draft adds channels. Hence the original thesis, that steps would rent depth and not width, and so move WER while leaving SIM untouched. The data withdrew it as stated: read as a rate claim it is backwards, since WER in fact converges sooner than identity, not faster-for-longer. What survives is a magnitude statement, and only against measured floors — see coordinate-audit.html.

Layer-wise probing of speech models backs the split: speaker/acoustic attributes decode from early, shallow layers while content requires the deep compositional stack (Pasad et al.; cross-model analyses 2024–25). ASPECT-D tests whether the same anisotropy governs generation, with steps as the third axis.

The selectivity question — which capabilities can test-time compute buy? — is unasked even in text, where per-metric decomposition is harder to define. Speech hands us two cleanly separable capabilities with standard automatic metrics.

§3Mechanism in one picture

§4Formalization

Msep: errm(w, d, T) = Em + Am·w−αm + Bm·d−βm + Cm·T−τm
Msub: errm(w, d, T) = Em + Am·w−αm + Bm·(d·Tκm)−βm

H-D2 (primary): Δτ = τWER − τSIM > 0 — steps are metric-selective. H-D3 (primary, not supported): κWER > 0 and κWER > κSIM with Msub competitive on AICc — a measurable depth–step exchange rate for intelligibility. Msub was decisively worse (ΔAICc +69.3 WER / +71.3 SIM-o), so no κ exists to quote. H-D1 (secondary): the fixed-T anisotropy Δρ = αSIM/βSIM − αWER/βWER > 0. H-D4: small-budget fits extrapolate to the largest budget. Full tests, bootstrap scheme (run-level resampling — the 5 T-points of one run share a trained model), and the four pre-registered outcome classes are in protocol §7.

§5Design: one grid, a free surface

Training: the validated iso-N shape grid — budgets 20M/50M/125M (N = 12·d·w², head_dim 64), five aspect ratios each, aspect span matched across budgets, μP for LR transfer, everything else identical between runs. Seeds: 2 mandatory + 1 stretch (calendar-gated). The T axis costs no training: T is swept only at synthesis, T ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16} steps per codebook level with generation RNG fixed across T — so within an item, T is the only difference. 15 configs × 5 T = 75 surface points per metric from at most 45 trained models.

Backbone recipe (frozen in configs/grid.json): bidirectional transformer over Mimi tokens, SoundStorm-style coarse-to-fine masked training, MaskGIT confidence decoding, no CFG, no duration model — total length from a corpus char-rate estimate, the conditioning strategy MaskGCT validated at scale. No speaker encoder: prompt continuation is the only identity pathway, so backbone shape is the only variable.

§6Known failure modes, planned for

The T-points of one run are correlated. They share a trained model, so naive point-wise bootstrap would overstate certainty. All resampling happens at the run level (protocol §7.2). This is the statistical detail most likely to be botched; it is frozen in writing.
"Steps do nothing" must be separable from "steps aren't wired." A sampler-integrity check (outputs must differ across T under matched RNG) runs before any metric; genuinely flat step-curves after a passing check are a finding (outcome F1), not a bug.
CFG is excluded on purpose. Guidance strength is a second inference-time axis that interacts with both metrics; v1 freezes it off. It is the first listed follow-up, not a silent knob.
LR confounding across shapes. μP for width, explicit depth-transfer gate, per-depth fallback; nothing tuned per shape or per T after Phase 1.
Floor/ceiling regimes. Pilot gate G2 checks intelligibility/similarity floors at T=16 before the grid launches; ladder-shift and recipe pivots each fire at most once; the recipe pivot stays inside the diffusion-LM family because the venue is DiffuLM.
The deadline is a failure mode. Unique to this project: calendar gate G6 with dated milestones, an ordered scope-cut list, and a writing floor — the paper starts by Aug 26 no matter what state the grid is in.

§7The exact benefit

B1 · A workshop-shaped first: capability-selective test-time scaling If H-D2 holds, "more denoising steps" is not a uniform quality dial — it is a targeted intelligibility dial. That lands in all three DiffuLM tracks at once: a scaling law over architecture and steps (Foundations), a sampling/serving allocation result (Efficiency), and quantitative evidence about what global self-correction can and cannot fix (Reasoning). The null is equally at home there: "refinement lifts all metrics equally" is a clean negative about self-correction selectivity.
B2 · κ is a serving decision rule Serial latency of masked decoding is 8·T·d·clayer(w). Conditional on H-D3, a fitted κ would answer whether to spend a latency budget on owned depth or rented steps. Outcome: H-D3 was not supported (ΔAICc(Msub − Msep) = +69.3 WER / +71.3 SIM-o against a ≤ +4 bar), so no κ was fitted and no κ-derived serving rule is shipped. results.html §4 reports budget-constrained minima on the measured grid instead — with T censored at 16, so they support no depth-versus-steps ordering. Plus the SIM corollary: identity is bought with width at zero serial-latency cost.
B3 · The serving contrast with AR, quantified on the same tokens Same Mimi tokens, same data as the AR ASPECT program: masked decoding carries no per-token KV growth (activations recomputed per pass) while AR KV grows linearly at d·w per frame. ASPECT-D's latency/quality frontier per metric, next to AR's KV-bound frontier, is the first same-tokenizer comparison of the two serving regimes' quality-per-serial-second — directly relevant to the workshop's long-context generation and serving topic.

§8Cost & calendar

Projected 240–420 B200-equivalent GPU-hours (hard cap 500, gate G5), which is a few days of wall-clock on available hardware. The binding constraint is the calendar, not compute: milestones M1–M6 with an ordered cut list run to the Aug 29 AoE wall (gate G6, protocol §8–9). Designed end state on the worst path: a smaller but complete grid, honest limitations, submitted on time.
