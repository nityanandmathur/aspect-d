## What this covers

- The trained model weights (`model.safetensors` and the accompanying `config.json` /
  `run.json`) in the Hugging Face repository
  [`nityanandmathur/aspect-d-masked-diffusion-tts`](https://huggingface.co/nityanandmathur/aspect-d-masked-diffusion-tts).
- Audio generated by those models, including `docs/samples/*_T1.flac` and `docs/samples/*_T16.flac`.
- Audio excerpted from the training corpus: `docs/samples/*_prompt.flac` and
  `docs/samples/*_target_gt.flac` are clips of real Emilia speakers.
- `results/runs-v1.5/f5tts_anchor/synth_T32/*.flac`, produced by the third-party F5-TTS v1 Base
  model, whose weights are themselves CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Dataset-derived metadata: eval-item transcripts and ids (for example `docs/samples/manifest.json`),
  `phone_vocab.json` and `dataset.json`.

## Why CC BY-NC 4.0

| Input | How it enters the release | License |
|---|---|---|
| Emilia (`amphion/Emilia-Dataset`, `Emilia/EN/` shards): the 2,000 h training subset | training data for every released model; source of the sample prompts and targets | **CC BY-NC 4.0**: non-commercial use only |
| Emilia-YODAS (`Emilia-YODAS/EN/` shards): used only by the scale-up corpus in `src/fetch_xl.py`, together with Emilia | training data for the scale-up run only | CC BY 4.0 |
| Mimi codec (`kyutai/mimi`) | the models predict Mimi tokens, and audio is decoded with Mimi | CC BY 4.0: attribution to Kyutai |
| F5-TTS v1 Base (`SWivid/F5-TTS`) | used only to generate the external-anchor audio | CC BY-NC 4.0 |

The evaluation models (Whisper, WavLM/UniSpeech SV, UTMOS, ECAPA, GE2E, wav2vec 2.0) are
used only to score audio. None of them is redistributed here.

Emilia's dataset card states that Emilia does not own the copyright of the audio: the
copyright stays with the owners of the original videos and recordings. The
`*_prompt.flac` and `*_target_gt.flac` clips are therefore redistributed only under
Emilia's non-commercial research terms. A rights holder who wants a clip removed can
open an issue on the repository.

## Intended use

These models are released for research on speech generation. Please do not use them to
impersonate a real person, or to clone a voice without its owner's consent. (This is a
request, not an additional license term.)
