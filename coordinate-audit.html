A headline that was a property of the coordinate

The v1.0 primary result was an exponent contrast, Δτ = τWER − τSIM. Refitting the identical measurements after a monotone change of the error variable sends it negative. This page records what failed, what replaced it, and what independently survived.

What changed Δτ is invariant to affine rescalings of the error — which the pre-registration checked — but not to monotone non-affine ones. Three of the five reparameterisations tried flip its sign. The paper now leads with a model-free, floor-referenced contrast whose value moves by less than 11% across the same maps.

2The replacement

Referenced to floors we measure — the ASR word error on the real recordings (0.0345) and the speaker similarity of a codec round trip (0.5554) — rather than to zero. From T=1 to T=16, refinement closes:

headline intelligibility 86.2% [82.8, 89.1] of the reachable range  ·  identity 46.4% [45.0, 47.8]  ·  ratio 1.86×

Because it is a ratio of differences on one axis, a monotone remap moves numerator and denominator together. Under the maps that flip Δτ's sign, the ratio does not move:

3What independently survived

The paper's top stated limitation was that selector and scorer shared the ECAPA-TDNN lineage. Five encoders across four families rescored the same clips. Gate G0(c) rejects three of them on absolute cosine scale despite AUC ≥ 0.983, so agreement is measured by per-item win rate, which is invariant to any monotone rescaling of an encoder's cosine.
