\section{Other inference-time interventions}
\label{app:interventions}
All arms in this appendix use the \NcrGamRuns\ runs of the \Nbudgetc\ budget and change one thing at
inference: the sampler's logits, the target length, the prompt length, or the split of
refinement passes across codebook levels.

\paragraph{Guidance and rate matching.}
Speaker-contrastive guidance is training-free. At each step it computes a second set of
logits with another item's prompt and pushes the output away from them with weight $\gamma$.
That second forward pass doubles the NFE, so guided $T{=}16$ is compared with unguided
$T{=}32$, at 256 NFE each. Rate matching sets each item's target length from the speaking
rate of its own prompt, in place of the corpus rate. Table~\ref{tab:negative} gives both.
Guidance raises SIM-o and costs WER. Rate matching gives a small mean gain that is not
reliable item by item.

\paragraph{Prompt length.}
Training used 3\,s prompts only. Table~\ref{tab:context} sweeps the prompt length at
inference, and every arm is scored against the same 3\,s reference. No length raises SIM-o
above the 3\,s arm. At 6 and 9\,s, SIM-o stays close to the 3\,s arm while absolute WER rises
to \NctxCwer\ and \NctxDwer: the output keeps the speaker but no longer follows the text.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering\small
  \caption{Prompt length at inference (15 runs, \NcrCtxItems\ items). Every arm is scored against the
  same 3\,s reference; WER is absolute.}

    prompt & SIM-o & WER \\
    \midrule

    6\,s & \NctxCsim & \NctxCwer \\

\end{table}

\paragraph{Allocating refinement passes across codebook levels.}
\label{app:alloc}
Table~\ref{tab:alloc} holds the total at 32 NFE and moves the passes between the eight
codebook levels. Coarse spends 25 passes on level 1 and one on each other level, fine does
the reverse, and uniform gives every level four. The same \NevalItems\ items are synthesised by every
schedule, and the bootstrap (\Nbootreps\ replicates) resamples items. Coarse minus fine is
$\NnfeCFdiff$ in absolute WER (CI $\NnfeCFci$). Against uniform, coarse has lower WER but also
lower SIM-o and UTMOS, so the split of passes trades intelligibility against identity.
