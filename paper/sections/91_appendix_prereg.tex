\section{Pre-registration ledger}
\label{app:prereg}
Table~\ref{tab:prereg} lists the hypotheses of the first three registrations and their
verdicts. Each registration was committed before any data that test it existed: v1.0 (H-D)
before the first training run, v1.1 (H-E) and v1.2 (H-S) before their extension runs. A
verdict of \textsc{discordant} means the hypothesis's two registered tests disagree in
direction.

Some registered items have no row. H-S0 classifies the codec headroom rather than testing a
claim; its value is the headroom in \S\ref{sec:identity}. H-E6, on confidence-adaptive
stopping, was gated on the compute schedule and not run. Two v1.3 hypotheses are supported:
search still beats refinement on the \NmaxNfour\,M and 90k-step checkpoints (H-T1), and
SIM-o still rises from 90k to 180k steps (H-T2). The third (H-T3) was withdrawn, because a code error meant the retrained model never saw the
intended variable-length prompts. The v1.5 guidance check is reported in
\S\ref{sec:limits}. The share of the reachable range (\S\ref{sec:gapclosed}) is not a
registered test: it was introduced at v1.4, after the registered analysis.
